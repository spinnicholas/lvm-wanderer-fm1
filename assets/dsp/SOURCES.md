## Wanderer 1.7: JIANT and Skiff investigation

Skiff, illuminus5, pinned revision:
https://github.com/illuminus5/skiff/tree/48e77fda5916e9a5ba9e7339161f719d8877ce49

`firmware/src/fm6_core.c` is the Apache-2.0 MSFA/Dexed C port by Google Inc.,
Pascal Gauthier and Leo Kuroshita / Hügelton Instruments. Wanderer's adapted
copy is `firmware/src/wanderer_fm6_core.c`. Its circular sine lookup and signed
arithmetic are adjusted, with the original author and Apache notices retained.
The full licence is `assets/dsp/Apache-2.0-msfa.txt`. The table generator is
adapted from `tools/gen_tables.py` (GPL-3.0-only, Leo Kuroshita). The glue,
ADSR calibration, quarter-ratio table and twenty original voices are Wanderer's.
No DX factory bank or manufacturer preset is included.

The band-limited oscillator restart is adapted from Skiff's
`firmware/src/eng_analog.c` (GPL-3.0-only, Leo Kuroshita / Hügelton Instruments).
Its separate bus storage, room diffusers, insert effects and parameter-aware
presets were also studied in `fx.c`, `eng_fm6.c` and `voice.c`.

JIANT, Juan Ignacio Antonucci, pinned revision:
https://github.com/juanjiant-bit/JIANT/tree/07d3d70de1a1bf19480119f607402c106989da54

Studied `eng_analog.c`, `eng_fm6.c`, `voice.c` and `fx.c`: oscillator interactions,
native FM, modulation and independent colour/feedback paths. No JIANT preset
bank, artwork or code is copied. Its architectural ideas informed the new
ring modulation and effects routing. The effect code is Wanderer's; common
DSP formulas do not imply measured circuit emulation.

On November 24, 2026 (US Central time), Nicholas James of Low Vision Music confirmed that Wanderer 3.1 RC1 is tested on his physical M-VAVE FM-1: **"its fully tested"**. This is the firmware author's report, not independent certification. Details of individual test steps were not supplied, so no specific measurements or migration routes are attributed to this test.
